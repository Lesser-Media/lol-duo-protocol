01 / CHAMP SELECT

WIN THE
DRAFT.

Use pick order to keep options open. Lock your lanes only when you have information worth acting on.

PICK ORDER PROTOCOL01—03
01

Offer the safer pick first

If you have an early slot, favor Wukong jungle, Ornn top, Jinx bot or your most practiced Vex mid. Avoid revealing Rammus into unknown magic damage or Veigar bot into unknown dive.

02

Trade for the useful last pick

If Duo Two is Top, give him the later slot when possible. In Mid + Jungle, preserve the slot for the stronger counterpick need. In ADC + Jungle, Wukong can lock early while Duo One waits for the enemy bot lane.

03

Check damage and engage

Vex, Ekko, Xerath and Veigar are magic damage. If you lock Veigar bot, ask for Wukong jungle or Garen top and confirm a teammate provides sustained physical damage. Keep Jinx when your team already skews AP.

04 / MAP CONTROL

TURN PRESSURE
INTO NEXUS.

Each successful fight should change the map. Follow the conversion path that matches your waves and death timers.

THE CONVERSION LOOPLIVE MAP
WAVE → VISION → NUMBERS → OBJECTIVE
01 / VISION + ROAMS

MOVE ON A CRASH.

Mid pushes the wave first, then moves with jungle into the river. Ward an entrance or a Faelight; don't walk blind for a ward. If the enemy mid can follow first, ping the danger and stay on the wave. ADC roams only after a safe crash or lane swap—not through a losing wave.

CHECK: WAVE PRIORITY · JUNGLE LOCATION · EXIT ROUTE
02 / TOWERS + OBJECTIVES

TAKE WHAT IS FREE.

After a kill, ask: tower, plates, dragon, grubs, Herald or reset? Favor the nearby objective you can finish before respawns. In 2026, plates remain on outer, inner and inhibitor towers, so even partial tower damage has value. Set the next wave before starting a contested epic monster.

CHECK: SMITE · WAVE · RESPAWN · ENEMY VISION
03 / SPLIT OR GROUP

SIDE LANE WITH A PURPOSE.

Let Garen or Ekko catch a side wave when they can escape or win the duel, and the other four can hold mid. Ornn, Wukong and Rammus should usually group for engage when a major objective is live. Jinx and Veigar stay with protection. Do not split if your team will be forced into a 4v5.

CHECK: TELEPORT · 1V1 · OBJECTIVE TIMER · WHO SHOWS
04 / INHIBITOR → NEXUS

FINISH THE JOB.

Take an inhibitor when it enables a real push or pressures the opposite side before Baron / Dragon. Don’t take a lone early inhibitor that only feeds safe farm and leaves you no follow-up. With Baron, push multiple waves, break towers, then hit Nexus turrets. If enemy carries are dead long enough and a wave is in base, end instead of resetting.

CHECK: LIVE WAVE · DEATH TIMERS · NEXUS TURRETS
05 / WHEN THE GAME GETS MESSY

THE RIGHT
RESPONSE.

Two quick decision trees for the states that throw the most games.

WHEN BEHIND

STOP THE BLEED.

  1. Trade sides. If they stack five for dragon and you cannot contest, take a safe cross-map wave or tower damage.
  2. Defend vision in layers. Ward your jungle entrances and Faelights from safety; do not face-check for a doomed objective.
  3. Catch waves, preserve carries. Jinx/Veigar farm under cover; Wukong/Ornn hold engage for an overextension.
  4. Fight on a spike. Wait for completed items, a numbers edge, or an enemy mistake. A late Smite steal is a contingency, not the base plan.
WHEN AHEAD

MAKE THEM ANSWER.

  1. Push mid first. It gives your duo a short route to either side of the map.
  2. Set vision before spawn. Be in the area early; clear entrances and place a ward on a Faelight if it covers the next fight.
  3. Threaten two things. Side wave plus Baron, or mid tower plus dragon. Force a choice.
  4. Convert cleanly. Win fight → push live wave → tower / inhibitor → Nexus if timers allow. Don't farm camps while a free structure stands.
